The Ritual of Care

The Candle Care Guide

Hand-poured soy wax candles are alive with botanical character. A few intentional rituals will keep your flame steady, your fragrance pure, and your candle burning evenly for up to 45 hours.

The First Burn Ritual Safety Principles
  • Trim to 5 mmBefore every single light
  • Melt to the EdgeEspecially on the first burn
  • 2–4 Hours MaxPrevents glass overheating
  • Keep the VesselEasy to clean & repurpose

Best Practices

The Four Golden Rituals

Follow these four simple steps every time you light your Scentavelle candle.

01

The First Burn Sets the Memory

Soy wax has a chemical memory. The very first time you light your candle, allow it to burn continuously for 2 to 3 hours until the melted wax pool reaches the glass edges on all sides.

Why it matters: If extinguished too early, the candle will "tunnel" downward, leaving unused wax stuck to the jar walls for every future burn.
02

Trim the Wick to 5 mm Every Time

Before each lighting, trim the cotton wick to approximately 5 mm (1/4 inch) using a wick trimmer or small scissors. Discard the trimmed wick tip outside the wax pool.

Why it matters: A shorter wick produces a steady, soot-free flame, prevents black smoke, and eliminates the "mushrooming" carbon bulb that causes crackling.
03

Respect the 4-Hour Ceiling

Never burn any candle for more than 4 hours in a single session. Give the wax at least 2 hours to cool and solidify before lighting it again.

Why it matters: Extended burns overheat the jar vessel, evaporate delicate fragrance notes too quickly, and cause the wick to drift off-centre.
04

Snuff Gently, Never Blow Out

Extinguish your flame by using a bell candle snuffer or by dipping the wick tip into the liquid melt pool with a tool, then gently re-centring it upright immediately.

Why it matters: Blowing out a flame generates harsh acrid smoke that ruins the ambient scent, and may splash hot melted wax onto surfaces.

Preparation

Preparing Your Candle Space

Before lighting, always inspect your candle. Place it on a stable, flat, heat-resistant surface such as marble, slate, stone, or hardwood with a coaster.

  • Keep away from air conditioners, ceiling fans, open windows, and heavy drafts. Air currents make the flame flicker and soot.
  • Ensure the wick is upright and centred in the middle of the wax before striking a match.
  • Never place burning candles within 10 cm (4 inches) of one another.

Storage

Storing Your Candles Between Burns

Candles made with natural soy wax and premium fragrance oils are sensitive to temperature and ambient sunlight. Proper storage preserves both scent integrity and appearance.

  • Keep cool & dry: Store candles at room temperature (18°C–25°C) away from direct sun, which can discolor wax or melt the surface.
  • Use the lid or dust cover: Keep the jar covered between burns to prevent airborne dust and hair from settling into the wax pool.
  • Natural frosting is normal: 100% soy wax often develops a slight velvety crystallization ("frosting"). This is a natural botanical hallmark of pure soy and does not affect burn quality or scent throw.

End of Life

When to Stop Burning & Repurposing the Glass

For your safety, discontinue burning when 10 mm (approx. 1/2 inch) of wax remains at the bottom of the glass. Burning past this point can overheat the base of the jar, creating heat stress against surfaces.

Our heavy amber, frosted, and smoked glass jars are designed to be cherished for years after the candle has given its last light.

How to safely clean your Scentavelle jar:

  1. Pour warm (not boiling) soapy water into the cooled vessel to soften remaining wax.
  2. Carefully pop out the metal wick sustainer clip with a spoon.
  3. Wipe clean with a soft paper towel and wash with warm water and gentle soap.
  4. Air dry thoroughly.

Ways to Repurpose:

  • Luxury makeup brush holder
  • Elegant brass pen & desk caddy
  • Small succulent or botanical planter
  • Cotton pad or bathroom vanity holder

Responsible Care

Candle Safety Principles

Fire is an element of comfort and beauty when treated with mindfulness and respect.

Never Leave Unattended

Always extinguish your candle before leaving the room, retiring to bed, or leaving the house. Never sleep with an open flame.

Keep Away from Pets & Children

Place candles well out of reach of curious paws and little hands, and never near a tabletop edge where they could be knocked over.

Clear Flammable Materials

Never burn near curtains, books, paper, loose blankets, dried florals, or flammable surfaces. Allow 1 metre of clear headroom above the flame.

Do Not Move While Hot

Never handle or relocate a candle while the wax is liquid or the glass is hot. Allow the vessel to cool completely before touching.

Solutions

Candle Troubleshooting

My candle has tunnelled down the centre. How do I fix it?

Wrap a layer of aluminium foil around the top rim of the jar like a dome, leaving an opening at the top for oxygen and the flame. Light the candle carefully and let it burn for 1–2 hours. The trapped heat will gently liquefy the hardened wax on the sides, resetting the melt pool.

Why is the flame flickering or producing dark smoke?

Extinguish the candle immediately. This happens either because the wick is too long (greater than 5 mm) or because the candle is placed in a draft from a fan or window. Trim the wick, relocate the candle to still air, and relight.

Why does the wax surface look uneven or bumpy after burning?

This is standard behavior for pure, additive-free soy wax. Soy wax cools at varying rates depending on ambient temperature, creating unique textures. It is visual proof that your candle contains genuine plant wax with zero synthetic hardeners, and it will melt completely flat on the next burn.
