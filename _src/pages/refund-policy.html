Fairness & Customer Care

Refund & Replacement Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Hand-poured candles are fragile personal fragrance products. Our policy is designed to be completely fair, prompt, and transparent.

1. Damaged on Arrival (100% Free Replacement)

We pack every candle with multiple layers of shockproof cushioning. However, if transit mishandling causes your candle vessel to arrive chipped, broken, or cracked, you are entitled to a free replacement at zero cost to you.

What to do:

  1. Take clear photographs or a short video showing the outer shipping box and the damaged candle.
  2. Send the photos to Mahesh on WhatsApp (+91 75694 03967) within 48 hours of delivery.
  3. Once verified, we will dispatch a brand-new replacement candle within 24 to 48 hours. If the specific fragrance is temporarily out of stock, you may choose an alternative scent or request a full refund.

2. Wrong or Missing Items

If you receive a different candle variant than what was confirmed, or if an item is missing from your gift set, notify Mahesh within 48 hours of delivery. We will promptly courier the correct item to you at no additional shipping charge.

3. Non-Returnable Situations

Due to hygiene, fire safety, and the personal nature of home fragrances, we cannot accept returns or provide refunds under the following circumstances:

  • Candles that have already been lighted, burnt, or altered.
  • Personal scent preference (e.g. deciding after delivery that you do not personally prefer floral or woody notes). We recommend trying our Scent Discovery Set first if you are unsure of your fragrance profile!
  • Damage reported after the 48-hour delivery inspection window.
  • Items damaged due to failure to follow our Candle Care Guide (e.g., untrimmed wicks or burning longer than 4 hours).

4. Order Cancellations Prior to Dispatch

If you change your mind, you may cancel your order at any time before it has been dispatched with our courier partner. Simply message Mahesh on WhatsApp. If payment was already completed, we will issue a full refund to your original payment method within 24 hours.

5. Refund Processing

Where a refund is approved (e.g., transit breakage where a replacement cannot be provided, or cancellation prior to dispatch), refunds are processed directly via original UPI or bank transfer within 24 to 48 hours.
