Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our candles, ingredients, hand-poured craft, ordering, and delivery.

How long do the candles burn?

Our standard 220g candles burn for 35 to 45 hours when cared for properly. To achieve the longest burn time, trim the wick to 5 mm before every burn and avoid burning for more than 4 hours in a single session. Mini discovery candles burn for approximately 12–15 hours each.

What wax do you use?

We use a custom, hand-formulated premium soy wax blend paired with lead-free braided cotton wicks. Our blend is clean-burning, releases fragrance evenly into your room, and is 100% free of paraffin, parabens, phthalates, and animal by-products.

Are the candles hand-poured?

Yes, every single Scentavelle candle is hand-poured in small batches in India. Hand-pouring allows us to monitor pouring temperatures precisely, inspect every cotton wick for central placement, and ensure that every vessel meets our luxury standards.

How strong are the fragrances?

We calibrate our candles for a refined, ambient scent throw. They are designed to transform the mood of a room gently without triggering headaches or overpowering your senses. For larger living spaces, richer blends like Velvet Oud or Amber Chai are ideal; for bedrooms and studies, softer scents like Lavender Veil and Vanilla Dusk provide calm presence.

Yes! For orders of 30 pieces or more, we offer custom-designed candle labels featuring your brand logo, event name, couple monogram, date, or personal quote. We share digital mockups for your approval before pouring.

Orders are dispatched within 24 to 48 hours. Delivery typically takes 3 to 5 business days for metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata) and 4 to 7 business days for the rest of India. You will receive WhatsApp tracking details once your package is dispatched.

What happens if my candle arrives damaged?

While we pack every candle in heavy bubble wrap and reinforced outer cartons, courier transit can occasionally cause breakage. If your candle arrives damaged, simply take a photo of the package and glass within 48 hours and send it to Mahesh on WhatsApp. We will dispatch a free replacement immediately at zero cost to you.

What is your replacement/refund policy?

Because candles are fragile personal fragrance products, we do not accept returns for used or lighted candles. However, for any damaged, defective, or incorrect products received, we provide a 100% free replacement or full refund upon photo verification. Read our full Refund & Replacement Policy for details.
