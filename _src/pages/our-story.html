The Scentavelle Philosophy

A little light.
A lasting memory.

Scents for a softer life.

Scentavelle was created around a simple idea — that the smallest moments can feel extraordinary when we slow down enough to experience them.

A candle is a small thing. But light one at the end of a long day and a room changes: the light turns golden, the air softens, and for a little while nothing needs to be hurried.

Every fragrance is designed to transform the atmosphere around you, from quiet mornings and slow evenings to celebrations shared with people you love.

The craft

Made slowly, poured by hand

Our candles are hand-poured in India, in small batches, using a premium soy wax blend, cotton wicks and carefully chosen fragrance oils.

We balance each fragrance so it fills a room gently — present, but never overwhelming — and we choose vessels that are beautiful enough to keep long after the candle is finished.
