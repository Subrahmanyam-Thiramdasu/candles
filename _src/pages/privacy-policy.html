Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. At Scentavelle, we treat your personal information with the same mindfulness and care that goes into hand-pouring our candles.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Scentavelle ("we", "our", or "us"), founded by Mahesh, collects, uses, and protects information when you browse our website (scentavelle.in) or communicate with us via WhatsApp.

We are a small-batch, direct-to-consumer Indian candle brand. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect the information necessary to serve you, prepare your candle orders, and answer your enquiries:

  • Contact Details: Your name, phone number, and optional email address when you submit an enquiry form, join the Scentavelle newsletter, or reach out to us.
  • Delivery Information: Your shipping address, city, and 6-digit postal pincode when you prepare a candle order.
  • WhatsApp Communications: Text messages, fragrance preferences, and order specifications that you share directly with Mahesh on WhatsApp.
  • Device Storage (localStorage): We use browser localStorage on your device to remember your shopping cart items and delivery form inputs between visits, so you don't lose your selections if you refresh your browser. No sensitive financial information is ever stored in localStorage.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your details are used solely for the following purposes:

  • To process, pack, and courier your candle orders to your designated address.
  • To send you dispatch notifications and courier tracking links via WhatsApp or SMS.
  • To respond personally to your fragrance questions, custom bulk order enquiries, or gift set requests.
  • To send occasional newsletter updates if you voluntarily subscribed to the Scentavelle Circle (you may unsubscribe at any time).

4. WhatsApp Order Enquiries & Payments

Our website utilizes a direct WhatsApp concierge flow. When you click "Order on WhatsApp", your browser opens a chat with Mahesh (+91 75694 03967) with your order summary pre-filled.

No card numbers, CVVs, net-banking passwords, or UPI PINs are ever collected, processed, or stored on this website. Any payment arrangements are confirmed securely via standard UPI / payment links provided directly by Mahesh during your chat.

5. Third-Party Services & Couriers

To safely deliver hand-poured candles to your doorstep across India, we share your delivery name, address, phone number, and pincode exclusively with reputable Indian courier partners (such as Delhivery, Bluedart, DTDC, or Speed Post). These partners use your data solely for shipping transit and delivery verification.

6. Data Security & Retention

We take all reasonable precautions to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. Order records are retained for a reasonable period to assist you with repeat orders, tracking queries, and accounting compliance, after which they are securely archived or purged.

7. Your Rights

You have the right at any time to:

  • Ask what contact details we have on file for you.
  • Request correction of any outdated or inaccurate delivery information.
  • Request deletion of your contact details from our records.
  • Opt out of any promotional communications.
