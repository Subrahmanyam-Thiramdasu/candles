Delivery & Logistics

Shipping Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Every Scentavelle candle is hand-packed with multi-layered protective cushioning so it arrives at your doorstep in pristine condition.

1. Pan-India Delivery Coverage

We provide Pan-India delivery to serviceable pincodes via trusted express logistics partners including Delhivery, Bluedart, DTDC, and India Post Speed Post.

2. Shipping Rates & Free Shipping Threshold

  • Orders Above ₹999: FREE Standard Shipping across India.
  • Orders Below ₹999: A nominal flat shipping charge of ₹99 is applied at checkout to cover courier handling and shockproof packaging.

3. Order Processing & Dispatch Time

Because our candles are produced in small batches, orders are carefully inspected, bubble-wrapped, and dispatched within 24 to 48 hours of order confirmation on business days (Monday through Saturday, excluding national holidays).

For custom bulk orders or corporate gifting orders with personalized labels, dispatch timelines typically range between 5 to 10 working days, as confirmed with Mahesh during your order discussion.

4. Estimated Delivery Timelines

Once dispatched from our studio, estimated transit times are as follows:

  • Metro Cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata): 3 to 5 business days.
  • Tier-2 & Tier-3 Cities: 4 to 6 business days.
  • Remote / Northeast / J&K / Island Locations: 6 to 9 business days depending on regional postal connectivity.

Please note: Delivery timelines are courier estimates. Occasional delays may arise due to regional weather disruptions, festive courier volume, or local logistics restrictions.

5. Shipment Tracking

As soon as your package is dispatched, Mahesh will send you your courier tracking link and AWB number via WhatsApp or SMS. You can monitor your candle’s journey directly on the courier partner's portal in real-time.

6. Packaging & Damage Protection

Glass candle vessels require exceptional care during transit. Every Scentavelle candle is wrapped in heavy bubble cushioning, secured with internal cardboard inserts, and boxed in a sturdy corrugated outer carton.

If your package arrives visibly broken or damaged by the courier, please take photos of the box and product within 48 hours and send them to Mahesh on WhatsApp (+91 75694 03967). We will dispatch a free replacement immediately.

7. Incorrect Address / Failed Deliveries

Please double-check your complete shipping address, landmark, and 6-digit pincode during checkout. In the event that a parcel is returned to us due to an incorrect address or non-availability of the recipient after multiple delivery attempts, re-shipping charges may apply.
